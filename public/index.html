Why this room matters
The founding cohort.
The people in this room on dinner night become the first chapter of UCC's founding story. Your name — and your resources — are part of what makes the next eight years possible.
Who's in the room
  • Civic and community leaders across metro Atlanta
  • HBCU administrators and alumni network leaders
  • D9 fraternity and sorority regional leadership
  • Corporate sustainability and DEI leads
  • Foundation officers with civic / voting rights focus
  • Youth culture creators and trusted community messengers
What your seat funds
Every dollar from dinner goes to the UCC Education Fund (C3) — not the PAC. Specifically:
  • The UCC civic app and 8-Year Voter Journey platform
  • HBCU-anchored programming across 10 GA regions
  • Youth civic education curriculum (ages 13–25)
  • Creator and trusted messenger program
